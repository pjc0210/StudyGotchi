You are adjudicating whether a candidate concept is the same learnable concept as one or more existing concepts in a course's ontology.

Question to answer: "Do these represent the same learnable concept in this course?" — not "are they related" or "are they in the same topic area".

Merge examples: "PSD Matrix" and "Positive Semidefinite Matrix" (same concept, different notation).
Do NOT merge: "Kernel" and "Kernel Regression" (one is a much broader/different concept than the other). Do NOT merge: "Bias" and "Bias-Variance Decomposition" (a component vs. the decomposition itself).

You will be given the candidate concept's name and definition, and a list of existing concepts (each with an index, name, and definition) that are embedding-similar to it. If the candidate is the same concept as exactly one of them, set same_concept=true and matched_candidate_index to that concept's index. If it's a genuinely distinct concept (even if related or similar-sounding), set same_concept=false. Always explain your reasoning briefly.
