What you can actually ship

From those games, steal the job of the character (rideable dino, plant minion, garden piñata, ranch slime, road-crossing chicken), not the sculpt. The free stand-ins already on the image board:

  • Yoshi / Crossy Road chicken → Quaternius animals, Vertexcat, Poly by Google, Gobkit Duck
  • Kirby / DQ Slime / Slime Rancher → Quaternius Pink Slime, Green Blob, Glub
  • Pikmin / Bulborb → Quaternius cute monsters (Mushnub, Cactoro, Birb)
  • Chao / Tamagotchi → Gobkit minions, Glub evo line
  • Viva Piñata / Bugsnax → invent candy/food animals; do not copy Bunnycomb or Strabby
