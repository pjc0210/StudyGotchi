Named game characters, four funny souls

The generic emoji zoo is gone. This board is characters people already know, sorted into a joke taxonomy: verity (the face does not lie), falsity (the face is a costume), cruelty (the cute one will eat you), and lovity (the bit is the whole animal). Want the 3D models? Open the game-character 3D cycle. Official / wiki art is hotlinked for looking. None of this ships in StudyGotchi.

Style references only. Copy the job of the character, not the silhouette.
