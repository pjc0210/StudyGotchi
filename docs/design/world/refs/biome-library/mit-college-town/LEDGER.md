# Academy Town (`academy-town`) reference ledger: MIT college town

Reference use is structural and compositional only: campus **structure** (a dome-fronted court opening to a river, one long connecting corridor of halls, a transit entrance on a square, a chapel, one sculptural "creative" building, a dorm street along the river) is borrowed as layout; every form in the biome is an original toybox low-poly design. No literal dome copy, no logos, no real names inside the world. Provenance is copied from `../SOURCES.tsv`; all files are official MIT publications or renders of them.
