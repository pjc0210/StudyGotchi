Toybox Low-Poly, in one line

Two registers that never mix. The world is chunky faceted 3D with no outlines and soft 3-step shading. The UI on top of it is a flat sticker: thick ink outline, cream fill, rounded, calm. Below, what each reference contributes.

Tile dioramas
Pedestal cliffs, one hero landmark per patch, 3-step shading, zero outlines. Our patch unit.
Toy creatures
Facets as a feature. Big head, tiny limbs, one saturated colour each, matte surfaces.
Low-poly animals
Lowest polygon budget we accept. Silhouette carries the species, not the detail.
Blob archetype
Dot eyes, flat mouth, no eyebrows. The face template for every blob and bean creature.
Bird archetype
A single faceted ball, a wedge beak, stick legs. Under 60 faces and still a kiwi.
Bean archetype
Loaf body, flat disc ears, pea nose. Two colours plus black. The bean body we use for rodents.
Sticker register (2D)
Thick dark-brown outline, flat fills, one belly highlight. This is the UI, never the 3D.
Soft-toy texture
Felt-like matte, pastel, deadpan faces. Tone reference for creature personalities.
Layered island
Stacked cake tiers with a crumbly rim. When a concept is demonstrated the ground rises like this.
Density and life
Many tiny creatures wandering, floating shelves, fog at the horizon. Target for a mature world.
Calm and colour
One flat sky, warm ground, cool water. Restraint reference: the world is never busy in colour.
Landmark progression
Isometric 3/4 view, one readable silhouette per object. Structure reference only, not the render style.
