Prepared from Philote's Tripo exports and the Blender edits in this Codex task.

- `tripo-creature-01` through `tripo-creature-47`: https://studio.tripo3d.ai/workspace/generate/collection-of-pokemon-inspired-creatures-with-varied-colors-and-shapes-87a91a9e-a5ec-4dbf-9305-28fce03a7cbc

Geometry derives from the separated and cleaned Blender source projects. Game conversion normalizes scale and facing, reduces geometry, bakes compact color textures, provides an alternate flat palette, and reuses StudyGotchi's node-animation curves. Creature 17 also has separation offcuts removed and a residual cyan separation mark recolored to match the purple surface.

No new ownership or license determination is made. The existing StudyGotchi source intake's reference-only designation remains in force. This package is not CC0 or first-party original artwork merely because its technical checks pass. The checker uses “first-party” as its default validation mode label, not as a rights determination.

Source Blender paths and final GLB SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the manifests. The game shaders and active roster are not modified by this handoff.
