# Music credits

Audition library for StudyGotchi. The website plays a shortlist from this folder; the rest stays here so we can thank the people who made them. The in-game chrome shows the current credit line and links here.

Huge WAVs (Cutie Pie, Shopping Theme) were transcoded to MP3 for the repo. Same compositions, same licenses. Sleepy and Ukulele Forest also ship as MP3 next to the original OGG because Safari cannot decode OGG Vorbis; the website plays the MP3s.

Changes we made (CC-BY asks us to say so): the 5:11 OGG mix (`lunarshopmix.ogg`) was cut to its first 72 bars (3:34) on the beat grid with a 60 ms crossfade at the seam so it loops, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, and transcoded to MP3 (`information-astral-bossa.mp3`).
