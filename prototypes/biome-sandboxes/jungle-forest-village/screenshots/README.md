QA iteration: the original circular canopy enclosure was removed. The final camera stays
south/front at 34–40° pitch and ±35° yaw; stations retarget without orbiting behind. The north/back
two-row skyline and tapered side wings alone receive horizon fog, while the foreground ground and
lagoon continue beneath the camera without a visible edge. Camera-relative fog keeps default and
max-dolly captures readable. District framing reports a 7.5% creature viewport height.

Art-direction QA replaced the initial tiny, repeated prop rings with region-specific silhouettes:
the overview now has low terrain patches and vegetation; the canopy district is a connected
multi-level inhabited network; the falls have three shelves and two visible drops; the lagoon has
varied houses, branching boardwalks, canoes, nets, and reeds; the ruin is broken/root-wrapped; and
the Riverworks wheel visibly intersects its sluice. Invisible picker meshes that produced polygon
outlines were removed, and station callouts were reduced to small translucent tags.
