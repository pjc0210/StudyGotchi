# Landscape Bouquet Marker Plan

**Goal:** Render every biome as an exuberant bouquet of oversized landscape elements growing from one visible circular plinth.

**Approved Ice direction:** Chilly Village Burst.

## Global grammar

- One low 12-facet circular plinth per marker; never stacked tiers.
- Keep a clear band of the plinth visible around the terrain.
- Build one connected, asymmetric terrain mass on the plinth.
- Enlarge the biome's signature structures until they overlap into one readable silhouette.
- Attach only each form's base to the terrain; curve the full form outward so every crown overhangs beyond the plinth edge.
- Use 2–4 elements in a reviewed priority order; progress reveals elements but does not add tiers.
- Use no signposts, world-space labels, or marker-to-biome morph.
- Preserve the fixed 2 px pass and review at 48, 96, and 512 px.
- Compare every marker directly with its references.

## Ice / Chilly Town

- Plinth: radius 16 m, height 2.4 m, cliff-ice side, 0.08 m snow cap.
- Terrain: six overlapping faceted snow lobes within radius 13 m, leaving a 3 m visible rim.
- Mountain: 32 m three-peak range across the back, with separate snow caps and exposed blue/lavender rock faces.
- Lighthouse: 28 m, front-left, three blue bands and an oversized red cap; its base attaches while the tower curves gently beyond the edge.
- Pines: 28 m cluster curving gently beyond the back-right edge.
- Houses: 24 m village cluster with paired gable roof panels, two windows, attic light, chimney, and snow caps.
- Creature: optional 4.5 m figure at the front edge.

## Review gate

1. Render Ice at 48 / 96 / 512 px in day and night.
2. Present it beside the Kirby W3 composition reference and Penguin Isle scale reference.
3. Record Philote's `keep / cut / change + parameter` decision.
4. Only after approval, build one family-specific bouquet composition for each remaining marker.

## Remaining implementation

For each of the other 16 rows:

1. Define a plinth material and connected terrain mass from the roster palette.
2. Choose one hero, one support, and up to two accent structures.
3. Exaggerate heights and lean while staying readable at 48 px.
4. Add family-specific rim treatment to the single plinth.
5. Verify day/night emissions.
6. Render the 96 px sheet and fix silhouette collisions without reintroducing tiers or slots.
